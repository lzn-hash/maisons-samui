Life in Koh Samui

A different horizon.
A familiar comfort.

An island you discover for its beaches, and choose for the life it makes possible.

Everyday life,
with an island rhythm.

Koh Samui leaves room for many rhythms of life: markets and local food, international restaurants, sport, the sea and quieter residential neighbourhoods.

Moving here also takes practical preparation. Schools, healthcare, transport and internet access: we encourage you to view the island through your everyday habits, not just your holidays.

01 · Everyday life

A table by the sea

Thai food, markets and memorable tables. Plenty of variety without losing the pleasure of simple things.

02 · Everyday life

Time for yourself

Yoga, Thai boxing, gyms, treatments or a swim. Outdoor life becomes part of your day again.

03 · Everyday life

Find your balance

For family life, choosing a neighbourhood goes hand in hand with schools, activities and healthcare.

04 · Everyday life

A different working day

For remote work, check the connection and workspace of the home. The surroundings do the rest.
