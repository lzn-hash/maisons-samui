The project

What is Latitude Samui?+

We develop a limited collection of villas in Koh Samui. Our team coordinates the project, construction and relationship with the future owner, alongside the relevant professionals.

Where are the villas located?+

Sabai, Suk, Jai and Baan Sawan are in the Lamai area. Thong Krut Village is a development in preparation in the south of the island.

Can I visit from abroad?+

Yes, a video presentation lets you explore the project and ask questions remotely. An in-person visit can then be arranged around your availability.

Buying

What framework applies to an overseas buyer?+

The acquisition framework depends on the buyer’s situation and the project. We arrange discussions with lawyers to clarify the proposed rights, contracts and due diligence before any decision.

What documents should I review before committing?+

Land documents, permissions, plans, specifications, contracts and financial terms are among the items to review with the appropriate professionals. The file depends on the project.

Does buying include a right to stay?+

Acquisition and residency are planned separately. For your move, we can direct you to a specialist to review your situation.

Construction

What is the construction timeline?+

The Baan Sawan, Suk and Jai listings show an indicative 12–18-month construction period. Sabai shows an estimated delivery in the fourth quarter of 2028. Your project’s schedule must be confirmed in the proposal and contractual documents.

What can I personalise?+

Choose an interior collection — Essentiel, Éveil des sens or Art de vivre — and an outdoor collection — Essentiel, Horizon or Art de vivre. Other adaptations are considered according to feasibility and quoted individually.

How do I follow construction remotely?+

The team organises progress updates and shares photographs. The format and frequency of communication are agreed together to keep a clear view of the stages.

What warranties are provided?+

Warranties, their scope, duration and conditions must be checked in your villa’s contractual documents. We explain these elements before any commitment.

Budget & payment

What is the starting price?+

Sabai, Suk and Jai start at €510,000, and Baan Sawan at €805,000, in the Essentiel configuration. Thong Krut Village pricing will be confirmed when the programme launches; it is currently available on request.

Is the villa delivered furnished?+

Essentiel includes a completed, equipped villa without furniture or decoration. Éveil des sens and Art de vivre add different levels of furnishing and design, quoted individually.

What additional costs should I plan for?+

Legal costs, acquisition fees and taxes, chosen options and running costs should be detailed for your situation. Request a complete proposal before comparing budgets.

How are payments organised?+

The schedule and terms are defined in the contract in relation to project milestones. Transfers in euros or baht and crypto payments through specialist providers are considered according to each transaction’s terms.

After handover

Can you arrange maintenance?+

Garden, pool, maintenance and concierge services can be arranged with local partners. Their scope and cost are defined separately.

What if I want to rent out my villa?+

Rental management can be discussed with specialist partners. The project should account for permitted use, management costs, seasonality and your own stays. No return is guaranteed.
