A village designed
as a whole.

The ambition is not to line up unrelated villas, but to create one coherent address: a shared architectural language, clear circulation and outdoor spaces that form a genuine residential landscape.

The masterplan is being studied for fifteen to twenty plots. More compact homes and repeatable construction choices help control costs without losing the Latitude identity.

The layout matters
as much as the homes.

On a site of this size, the road layout, plot subdivision, utilities and management of levels determine both the quality of life and the project’s economic balance.

The masterplan will seek the right balance between privacy, straightforward construction, tree retention and controlled retaining work. A small number of shared amenities remain under consideration.
