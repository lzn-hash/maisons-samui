Your buying journey

A new chapter.
Clear steps.

From our first conversation to receiving the keys, a continuing relationship and decisions you can understand.

01

Your project, your pace.

First conversation

We discuss your intended use, expectations, budget and timeline. A primary residence, holiday home and rental project each call for a different approach.

To clarify: how you want to use the villa.

02

In person or remotely.

Visit the project

A visit helps you understand the plot, orientation, access and surroundings. If you are abroad, we can arrange a video conversation to present the project and answer your questions.

To consider: the plot as much as the house.

03

Understand before signing.

Legal framework

Before any commitment, acquisition terms and project documents are reviewed with the appropriate professionals. Lawyers explain the options suited to your circumstances and the checks to carry out.

To clarify: rights, contracts, costs and timing.

04

Documented decisions.

Construction follow-up

Specifications, chosen collections and the payment schedule are defined for your villa. During construction, progress updates and photos help you follow the stages and decisions.

To follow: progress, choices and stage approvals.

05

Start your life on the island.

Receiving the keys

The handover gives you the opportunity to inspect the villa and its features against the agreed documents. Maintenance, pool, garden or management needs can then be arranged with local partners.

To organise: your arrival and the daily life of the home.

Price & payment

Know what is planned.
Before moving forward.

The starting price corresponds to the Carte Blanche specification. Furnishing options, acquisition costs and payment terms should appear in a detailed proposal.

Bank transfer in euros or baht, or cryptocurrency payment through specialist providers: availability, fees and conditions are confirmed for each project.
