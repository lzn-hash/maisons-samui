How information entered on this site is used.

Your enquiries

The contact flow prepares an enquiry using the details you enter: name, contact information, message and, where relevant, villa and selected options. In this version, the enquiry is prepared for your email app. It is not sent automatically.

If you choose to send it, Latitude Samui uses this information to reply and follow up on your enquiry. Contact contact@latitudesamui.com with any question about your information.

Preferences on your device

Your language choice is saved in your browser. Collection choices may be kept during your session so you can return to your configuration. These preferences are not transmitted by the configurator.

External services

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. The Odoo chat loads only when you activate it. Links to Google Maps and your email app open external services, which have their own terms.
