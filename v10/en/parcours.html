The steps to buying your home

  1. 01

    First conversation

    Start with the way you live.

    We discuss how you will use the home, your expectations, budget and timeline. A main home, holiday home and rental project each call for a different approach.

    Your priorities, your budget and your project’s pace.

  2. 02

    Visit the project

    Connect the images with reality.

    A visit lets you explore the plot, orientation, access and surroundings. Remotely, a video call can introduce the project and answer your first questions.

    The plot, the spaces and your everyday journeys.

  3. 03

    Legal framework

    Understand before committing.

    Purchase arrangements and project documents are reviewed with the relevant professionals. Lawyers explain the options suited to your circumstances and the checks to complete.

    The proposed rights, contracts, costs and timeline.

  4. 04

    Construction follow-up

    Move forward with documented choices.

    Specifications, selected collections and the payment schedule are defined for your villa. During construction, updates and photos help track progress and decisions.

    Progress, your choices and milestone approvals.

  5. 05

    Receiving the keys

    Take possession of your home.

    Handover provides an opportunity to inspect the villa and its features against the agreed documents. Maintenance, pool, garden or management needs can then be arranged with local partners.

    Handover, your arrival and life in your home.
