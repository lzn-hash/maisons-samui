at your own pace.

With family, as a couple, working remotely or preparing for retirement: your everyday life is the starting point. The home and its location follow from there.

With family

A home with room for children, a garden for time together and the beach at the end of the day. Your choice of neighbourhood also depends on school and everyday journeys.

Explore together: schools, activities and getting around.

Working remotely

Keep your professional projects, with a different view from your desk. For digital nomads and remote-working stays alike, a quiet workspace matters as much as the terrace.

Check at the villa: connectivity and workspace.

Settling as a couple

Find your favourite places, go out for dinner, explore the island and return home. A first pied-à-terre can become somewhere you stay longer, with space to welcome guests.

Define your plans: outings, stays and hosting loved ones.

Enjoying retirement

Take time to walk, garden and welcome family. For a longer-term move, the comfort of the home, its access and nearby services deserve particular attention.

Plan ahead: access, healthcare and home maintenance.

Preparing for life here
in detail.

A beautiful view matters. So does a home suited to everyday life. Here are the points to consider together before choosing.

What should you look at during a visit?+

Explore access roads, observe the orientation and try the routes you will use regularly. Visit the neighbourhood at different times of day to understand its rhythm.

How should you prepare for time away?+

The garden, pool and home need care between stays. Clarify maintenance needs and arrangements before you leave.

Where should you start when choosing a villa?+

Define the length of your stays, how many people you will host and which services you want nearby. These criteria help you compare villas beyond their photos.
