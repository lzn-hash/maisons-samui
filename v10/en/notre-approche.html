Villas designed for
life in Koh Samui.

Each villa begins with a plot, an orientation and a way of life. We look for flowing spaces, a natural relationship with the outdoors and choices that suit the tropical climate.

Personalisation then gives the home your character. Interior and outdoor collections are chosen separately, with detailed specifications and pricing tailored to your project.

A direct connection for
regular follow-up.

We coordinate villa development and construction monitoring with local teams and partners. Decisions made with you are communicated to those involved and followed through on site.

Architects, engineers, contractors and lawyers each work within their field. We connect their work to keep you informed and prepare upcoming decisions.

Your villa,
your way.

The same home can accommodate different ways of living. Personalisation covers furniture, materials, decoration and the garden, with options designed as a coherent whole.

Essentiel

An equipped, unfurnished villa. You remain free to bring your own furniture and objects.

Éveil des sens

A furnished proposal with coordinated furniture, lighting and textiles to start living in your home.

Art de vivre

A more complete design in which furniture, decoration and details create a more personal atmosphere.

independently.

Essentiel, Horizon or Art de vivre: three levels of planting and outdoor design to suit your use. You can combine different interior and outdoor collections.
